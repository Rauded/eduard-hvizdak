**Partly done: 2.1 hydration.** Two independent causes. The first, adjacent JSX
text nodes being welded into one by `outerHTML`, is fixed: the prerender now
writes React's own `<!-- -->` separators. The second is the `<Suspense>`

## 2. Bugs and defects, confirmed live

### 2.1 React hydration fails on every single page load (HIGH)
Confirmed in the production console on a cold load of `/`:

```
Minified React error #418  (text content mismatch during hydration)
Minified React error #423  (hydration failed, recovering with client render)
```

React discards the prerendered DOM and re-renders the whole root on the client.
The prerender step is doing SEO work but almost none of the perceived-speed work
it was added for. `AUDIT_TODO.md` flagged this as deferred pending a decision; it
is still firing. Root cause there: the prerender writes a plain DOM snapshot with
no SSR Suspense markers, so `hydrateRoot` cannot hydrate the `<Suspense>` boundary
that route code-splitting introduced.

Options, in order of how much I would trust them:
1. Render the hero shader client-only and move `<Suspense>` off the Home route, so
   the homepage (the page that matters) hydrates cleanly.
2. Move to real streaming SSR in `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
3. Drop route code-splitting (undoes a July win; not worth it).

### 2.2 25 MB of dead video in every deploy (HIGH, easy)
`src/components/portfolio/projectsData.tsx:12`

```ts
return require(`../../assets/projects/${path}`);
```

A template-literal `require` makes webpack build a context over the **entire**
`src/assets/projects/` tree, so `originals/` gets emitted into the build even
though nothing imports it:

| file | size |
| --- | --- |
| `kouzelnici-original.mp4` | 12.5 MB |
| `inzerpro-original.mp4` | 7.5 MB |
| `nasadclaw-original.mp4` | 4.9 MB |

Users never download these, but they are in every build and every deploy (build
dir is 96 MB). Fix: move `originals/` out of `src/` entirely (they are archive
masters, not site assets).

### 2.3 The inline CV PDF renders as a black box
The `/` Experience section embeds `EduardHvizdakCV.pdf` in a 585x955 iframe next
to the structured experience list. Scrolling past it leaves the PDF viewer
showing a solid black rectangle (screenshotted). It also duplicates content
already on the page in better form, and it is the heaviest single element in the
section. Remove the viewer, keep "Download CV" and "Open in new tab".

### 2.4 Two `<h1>` on the homepage
The prerendered SEO h1 ("Eduard Hvizdak, AI Engineer & Founder") and the hero h1
("I'm Eduard Hvizdak.") both ship. Pick one.

### 2.5 An unknown blog slug lands on `/blog` titled "Page not found"
`/blog/does-not-exist` ends up at `/blog` with a 404 title. Confusing state; it
should either render the 404 page at its own URL or redirect cleanly.

## 3. Performance: images are now the whole problem

Cold homepage is roughly **3.5 MB across 35 requests**. Every large item is an
image served far above the size it is displayed at:

| asset | size | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `youth-policy-dialogue.png` | 851 KB | 872x826 natural, displayed 386x366 |
| `newsmatics-hackathon-group.jpg` | 724 KB | used only as a video poster |
| StudyExe slideshow (6 PNGs) | ~1.3 MB | all fetched on the homepage, card closed |
| `picture_of_me.webp` | 262 KB | 1200x1599 natural, displayed 486x647 |

None of these have `srcset`. Converting to AVIF/WebP at 2x display width and
deferring the StudyExe slideshow until its card opens should take the homepage
from ~3.5 MB to well under 800 KB.

**Blog routes are worse.** A post page pulls **1.79 MB of `.ttf` fonts**:

| font | size |
| --- | --- |
| `SourceSerif4.ttf` | 1181 KB |
| `Inter-Regular.ttf` | 303 KB |
| `Inter-Bold.ttf` | 309 KB |

These are declared as `format('truetype')` in `src/index.css`. Everything else on
the site is already woff2. Converting these three (plus the 14 unused Roboto Mono
faces, which should probably just be deleted) is roughly a 70% cut, about 1.3 MB
off every blog page. Total blog post page today: 6.7 MB decoded.

Secondary: the local build's `main.js` is 779 KB raw. Worth a bundle-analyzer pass
after the above, not before.

## 4. Positioning and content: the real gap

The site currently proves **what Eduard built**. Strong professional sites also
prove **that other people vouch for it** and **what changed as a result**. Both
are close to absent here, and both are cheap to add.

### 4.1 There are zero testimonials (biggest single gap)
Not one quote from a colleague, client, or paying customer. Every credibility
guide converges on this and every consulting site that converts has it. Three
quotes of two sentences each, with a real name, role, and photo, from CZS, OneBond
or iGalileo, and one InzerPro customer, would move more than any design change on
this list.

### 4.2 The client logo wall already exists and is hidden
`/references` renders Artiffine, Masaryk University, OneBond, iGalileo, and EDUC
Alliance with logos and dates. It is:
- not linked from the nav,
- not linked from the footer,
- marked `noindex` in its own `<Seo>` call.

So the strongest ready-made proof on the site is unreachable. Put that logo row
on the homepage directly under the hero and drop the `noindex`.

### 4.3 Projects describe, they do not quantify
Six project cards, and almost none carry an outcome number. The numbers exist,
they are just scattered elsewhere: "millions of contracts", "200k+ students",
"844 search impressions / 28d", "paying customers". Give each card one metric
line under the title. That is the line between reading as a student gallery and
reading as a founder-engineer.

### 4.4 `/services` is a page-and-a-half of empty
The fold is a headline, a paragraph, one CTA, and then whitespace. For a page
meant to sell engagements it is missing: who it is for, three offers each with an
outcome, a three-step process, proof, a price anchor or typical engagement size,
and an FAQ. `services-page-draft.md` and `competitor-services-research.md` in the
parent directory already contain most of this thinking.

### 4.5 Craft details
- **Nav is 7 items each with an icon.** Icon-per-item reads busier than the rest
  of the design. The reference sites this one is modeled on use text-only nav.
- **Project body copy is monospace at paragraph length.** Monospace is a label
  face; three-line paragraphs in it are measurably harder to read.
- **The `TileWordmark` band** (342 px above the footer) renders very faint and
  spends a full screen-third on a decorative canvas. Either strengthen it or cut it.
